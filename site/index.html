SIDE A

WHAT IT
DOES.

A1 SMART RADIO It listens. It learns. It queues. On-device MFCC + chroma analysis, session mood-lock, skip affinity — a radio station running on your CPU. A2 PURE-RUST ENGINE Symphonia decode → Rubato resample → lock-free ring buffer → CPAL output. Progress and seek driven by real engine events, never timers. A3 INSTANT LIBRARY Tags, art and features extracted once, cached on disk. Restart re-scans hundreds of files in milliseconds. Search, sort, grid — instant. A4 SYNCED LYRICS Drop a .lrc next to the file and Now Playing turns karaoke — lines light up in time, locally rendered. A5 EQ + REPLAYGAIN 10 bands, 6 presets, your own curves — and loudness matched across the whole library so quiet cuts stop getting steamrolled. A6 DESKTOP-NATIVE MPRIS out of the box: media keys, playerctl, desktop widgets, Bluetooth. Light & dark, six palettes, full keyboard access.
